Privacy Policy

Official legal and operational terms for SPOUZY Clinics & Research Centres, a specialist wing of Problem Solver Group.

Effective Date: [Date]  ·  Last Updated: [Date]  ·  Jurisdiction: India

SPOUZY handles some of the most private information people ever share. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we protect it, and the rights you have. We act as a “Data Fiduciary” under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the DPDP Rules, 2025, and also follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules on sensitive personal data.

1. Data we collect

Identity & contactName or nickname, age, gender, phone, email, cityEnquiry, booking, account

We collect only what is needed for the service you request.

2. Why we use your data

  • To respond to enquiries and book, deliver and follow up on your services
  • To provide medical care, counselling and mediation, and keep the records required by law and professional standards
  • To process payments, refunds and guarantee claims
  • To send appointment reminders and service messages (discreetly worded)
  • To improve our services, using anonymised or aggregated data wherever possible
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests
  • With your separate consent only: to send newsletters, offers or webinar invitations

3. Legal basis: your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your free, specific, informed and unambiguous consent, given through a clear affirmative action such as ticking a box or confirming a booking. We may also process data without consent only where the DPDP Act permits it, for example in a medical emergency involving a threat to life, or to comply with law or a court order.

4. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to [privacy@spouzy.in], as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done. If you withdraw consent, we may be unable to continue some services, and we may need to keep certain records where the law requires it.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only:

  • Within SPOUZY, with the experts directly involved in your care, on a need-to-know basis
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf under written contracts and confidentiality obligations: [payment gateway], [video consultation platform], [cloud hosting / practice-management software], [SMS/WhatsApp messaging provider]
  • With a laboratory, pharmacy or another specialist only with your consent
  • With authorities where required by law, a court order, or to protect someone’s life or safety

We never share your information with your spouse, family members or employer without your written consent.

6. Data storage and transfer

Your data is stored on secure servers [in India / location]. If any data is processed outside India, we will do so only as permitted under the DPDP Act and with equivalent safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or as required by law. Medical records are kept for the period required under applicable medical regulations [state period, e.g. at least 3 years from the last consultation]. Counselling and mediation records are kept for [period]. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within [period]. After that, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. How we protect your data

We use reasonable security safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls limited to authorised staff, secure passwords and two-factor authentication, staff confidentiality agreements and training, and regular security reviews. Online sessions use encrypted platforms, and we do not record sessions without consent.

9. Personal data breach

If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Act and Rules, describing what happened, the likely impact and the steps being taken.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase data no longer needed, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity

To exercise these rights, write to [privacy@spouzy.in]. We will verify your identity before acting, and respond within [30] days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children’s data

We do not knowingly process personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian. We do not track children or use their data for targeted advertising.

See our Cookie Policy (B11).

13. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here with a new effective date, and notify you of significant changes by email or on the website.
