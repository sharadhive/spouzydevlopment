Sales & Client Coordination

Sales Coordinator

Coordinate incoming enquiries, organise follow-ups, and ensure every client receives timely, clear, and reassuring guidance.

Apply Now →

Key Responsibilities

  • Handle phone, email, and WhatsApp enquiries with discretion and professional warmth.
  • Schedule consultations, follow-ups, and appointments across our care network.
  • Maintain accurate client interaction records and CRM notes.
  • Liaise between clients, counsellors, and senior staff to ensure smooth service delivery.
  • Follow up with prospective clients who requested information or missed scheduled slots.
  • Prepare weekly summary reports on enquiry volumes, conversion, and common client queries.

Required Skills & Qualities

  • Excellent spoken and written English and Hindi communication.
  • Empathetic telephone manner and strong active-listening ability.
  • Good organisational skills with attention to detail and confidential record handling.
  • Familiarity with basic CRM tools, spreadsheets, and email suites.
  • High emotional intelligence when interacting with clients facing sensitive relationship challenges.

Preferred Background

  • Prior experience in client coordination, front-desk, hospitality, or healthcare customer care.
  • Comfort with multi-channel communication (voice, chat, email).
Compensation and employment terms are discussed during the selection process.
Marketing

Digital Marketing Executive

Plan, create, and publish educational content across digital channels, maintaining SPOUZY’s discreet, authoritative, and compassionate voice.

Apply Now →

Key Responsibilities

  • Draft and schedule social media posts, blog snippets, and newsletter content aligned with SPOUZY’s values.
  • Coordinate graphic design and short-form video assets with external or internal creatives.
  • Monitor audience engagement, comments, and messages with professional boundaries and prompt escalation where needed.
  • Assist with on-page SEO updates, meta tags, and content refreshes across the SPOUZY website.
  • Track basic organic traffic and engagement metrics to report on campaign performance.
  • Ensure all marketing communications comply with healthcare discretion and confidentiality standards.

Required Skills & Qualities

  • Strong written communication with a sensitive, refined, and non-sensational tone.
  • Familiarity with major social media platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook).
  • Working knowledge of Canva, basic photo/video editing tools, and content scheduling platforms.
  • Understanding of fundamental SEO principles and content management systems.
  • Respect for confidential healthcare and relationship wellness topics.

Preferred Background

  • Experience in content marketing, social media management, or copywriting for wellness, healthcare, or professional services.
  • Basic understanding of Google Analytics and Search Console.
Compensation and employment terms are discussed during the selection process.
Growth & Paid Media

Performance Marketing Specialist

Manage paid search and social campaigns to connect individuals and couples with the guidance and clinical care they need.

Apply Now →

Key Responsibilities

  • Build, monitor, and optimise paid advertising campaigns on Google Ads (Search, Display) and Meta (Facebook & Instagram).
  • Conduct keyword research, competitive analysis, and ad copy testing focused on high-intent healthcare and relationship queries.
  • Manage campaign budgets responsibly, tracking cost-per-lead (CPL) and return on ad spend (ROAS).
  • Collaborate on landing page conversion rate optimisation (CRO) and user journey improvements.
  • Set up and audit conversion tracking, tags, and analytics events via Google Tag Manager and GA4.
  • Deliver transparent weekly and monthly performance reports with actionable optimisation insights.

Required Skills & Qualities

  • Demonstrated hands-on experience managing Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns.
  • Strong analytical mindset with proficiency in spreadsheets and data visualisation.
  • In-depth understanding of ad policies regarding health, medical, and sensitive personal topics.
  • Knowledge of conversion tracking, UTM tagging, and attribution models.
  • Disciplined budget management and continuous A/B testing methodology.

Preferred Background

  • Prior experience driving qualified leads for healthcare clinics, mental wellness platforms, or professional consultancies.
  • Google Ads or Meta Blueprint certifications are an advantage.
Compensation and employment terms are discussed during the selection process.
