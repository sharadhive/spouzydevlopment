Reviews & Testimonials Policy

Official legal and operational terms for SPOUZY Clinics & Research Centres, a specialist wing of Problem Solver Group.

  • We publish only genuine feedback from real clients.
  • We publish a testimonial only with the client’s written consent, and anonymised (initials and city at most) unless the client asks otherwise in writing.
  • Clients may withdraw consent at any time, and we will remove the testimonial within [7] days.
  • We do not pay for, or offer discounts or gifts in exchange for, reviews.
  • We do not edit testimonials except to remove identifying details, with the client’s approval.
  • When replying to public online reviews, we never confirm that someone is a client or discuss their case.
