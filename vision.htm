OUR ETHICAL CHARTER

Bridging Clinical Science with Compassionate Discretion

For too long, issues of sexual inadequacy, marital dissatisfaction, and domestic friction have been shrouded in shame, misinformation, and fear of societal judgment. Couples suffered in silence until issues exploded into bitter divorces or chronic emotional distress.

Under the visionary guidance of Neilesh Jethmal and Problem Solver Group, SPOUZY is transforming this reality. We provide medical and counseling pathways that treat every patient with the highest clinical ethics, dignity, and absolute confidentiality.

Problem Solver Group Clinical & Ethical Principles

1. Primacy of Patient Well-Being

Our recommendations are guided exclusively by the psychological and medical best interests of our patients—free from commercial incentives.

2. Absolute Privilege & Secrecy

We treat all information shared during therapy and mediation sessions with ironclad legal and medical privilege.

3. Scientific Integrity & Transparency

We avoid quackery or pseudo-scientific claims. All intimacy and endocrine treatments are administered by board-certified physicians.

4. Inclusive, Non-Discriminatory Care

We provide welcoming, affirming support regardless of gender, sexual orientation, background, or socioeconomic standing.

SPOUZY is the specialized clinical and personal guidance wing of Problem Solver Group (PSG), established in 2004 by Neilesh Jethmal. We provide evidence-based marital counselling, intimacy therapy, and dignified dispute mediation.
