Do’s and Don’ts

Practical guidelines for navigating sensitive relationship conversations, respecting emotional boundaries, safeguarding personal privacy, and preparing for confidential professional care.

Respectful Communication

Do

  • Do express personal feelings using “I” statements rather than making accusations or generalizations.
  • Do listen with the genuine intent to understand before formulating your response.
  • Do agree to take a calm pause when emotions run high, setting a specific time to resume.

Don’t

  • Don’t bring up past resolved arguments to score points during a current disagreement.
  • Don’t interrupt, dismiss, or minimize your partner’s emotional perspective.
  • Don’t resort to stonewalling or silent treatment without communicating a need for space.

Boundaries & Emotional Safety

Do

  • Do communicate your personal boundaries and emotional bandwidth with clarity and kindness.
  • Do respect when a partner needs processing time before continuing a heavy topic.
  • Do recognize and protect each other’s individuality, friendships, and healthy autonomy.

Don’t

  • Don’t treat boundaries as punishments or ultimatums; they are parameters for mutual safety.
  • Don’t pressure anyone into decisions before they feel emotionally prepared and secure.
  • Don’t disregard or push past an explicit boundary once it has been shared.

Privacy, Discretion & Digital Safety

Do

  • Do keep intimate partner conversations and relationship decisions strictly between yourselves.
  • Do use confidential channels when seeking professional advice or scheduling consultations.
  • Do respect digital boundaries regarding phones, private messages, and location sharing.

Don’t

  • Don’t air private grievances or partner disagreements on public social media or group chats.
  • Don’t share details of private consultations without your partner’s explicit consent.
  • Don’t compromise trust by monitoring accounts or demanding access to private passwords.

Seeking Support & Preparing for Consultation

Do

  • Do note down the key patterns or questions you wish to explore ahead of your session.
  • Do consider an initial individual consultation if your partner is hesitant or not yet ready.
  • Do engage with your counsellor with honesty, openness, and readiness for self-reflection.

Don’t

  • Don’t wait until a relationship reaches breaking point; early guidance is gentler and more effective.
  • Don’t view counselling as a courtroom where a practitioner is expected to declare a winner.
  • Don’t hold back important medical or emotional context that can help your doctor or counsellor assist you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about SPOUZY care, appointments, and confidentiality:

How are SPOUZY and PSG connected?

SPOUZY is the relationship and intimate health wing of Problem Solver Group, founded by Neilesh Jethmal in 2004.

Which resources are free to use?

The student guide, ex-servicemen family guide, Do’s and Don’ts, blog, Gen Z Kama Sutra and homepage self-assessment are available to read or use without booking.

How can I join the team?

Open Join Our Team, select Doctor or Counsellor, and follow the professional enquiry link on that page.

Is our session strictly confidential?

Yes, absolutely. All sessions adhere to strict clinical confidentiality and legal privilege under our Confidentiality Pledge and Privacy Policy. We do not share records with any third parties, and anonymous consultation options are readily available.

Can I attend a relationship consultation alone if my partner isn't ready?

Yes. Over 40% of our clients begin with individual sessions to gain emotional clarity, process their own feelings, and learn practical strategies before introducing their partner when the time is right.

How do online video sessions work?

Upon booking, you receive a secure, encrypted one-click video room link. No special software download is required. Both partners can join from separate locations if living apart or traveling.

What qualifications do your doctors and counsellors hold?

Every specialist in the SPOUZY network is a certified medical practitioner (MD/MBBS), licensed clinical psychologist, or accredited family dispute mediator vetted under Problem Solver Group’s stringent credentialing committee.

Our clinical team reviews every question. Helpful answers are added to this page. Your email stays private and is never published.
