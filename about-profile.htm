A MESSAGE FROM THE FOUNDER

"Human dilemmas cannot be compartmentalized into mere legal files or medical charts."

When Neilesh Jethmal founded Problem Solver Group (PSG) in Mumbai in 2004, it was with a foundational realization: when a couple faces marital rupture, or an entrepreneur battles a corporate fallout, the issue is rarely just financial or legal. At its core, it is psychological, emotional, and intensely personal.

SPOUZY was established as PSG's specialized clinical and relationship guidance wing to provide an uncompromised sanctuary. Here, medical experts in andrology and endocrinology work hand-in-hand with clinical psychologists and veteran legal mediators—enabling individuals and couples to heal without public exposure or courtroom hostility.
