Reconnecting after time apart

Returning to everyday life together takes some adjustment, even when everyone has looked forward to it.

Ask about an ordinary day

Take turns describing a typical weekday while you were apart. What did mornings look like? Which task took longer than expected? These small details make changes easier to understand.

Try one routine together

Choose one ordinary thing to share this week, such as breakfast or planning errands. After a few days, ask what felt comfortable and what still needs adjusting.
